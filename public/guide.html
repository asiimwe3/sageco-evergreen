5 Tips for Getting the Most Out of SAGECO

🤖 Use the AI Broker First

Before contacting a human, chat with the AI Broker. It can search all properties, guide you through booking, and answer questions instantly — 24/7.

📱 Save to Home Screen

Open sageco-evergreen-co.vercel.app in your browser, tap the menu, and select "Add to Home Screen." It works like an app — no download needed.

🔒 Always Use Escrow

Never pay a broker directly. Always use SAGECO's booking and escrow system. Your money is protected until the viewing is confirmed.

Every broker has a WhatsApp button. Use it to ask about properties, negotiate, and get updates. Most brokers respond within hours.

Every property has GPS-verified boundaries. Check them before booking a viewing to make sure the location is right for you.
