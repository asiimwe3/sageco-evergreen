# SAGECO EVERGREEN — Facebook & Instagram Ads Strategy

## Campaign 1: Property Sales (Conversion)
**Objective:** Conversions (Landing page views / Contact)
**Budget:** UGX 50,000/day (~$13/day)
**Placement:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed/Stories, Messenger
**Target:** Uganda, ages 25-55

### Audience Targeting
**Core Audiences:**
- Location: Uganda (all cities), +Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono, Kyenjojo, Fort Portal
- Age: 25-55
- Interests: Real estate investing, Land lot, Property management, Investment, Agriculture, Business
- Behaviors: Mobile device users, Engaged shoppers

**Lookalike Audiences (create after pixel data):**
- Lookalike of website visitors (1-3%)
- Lookalike of people who booked viewings

**Custom Audiences:**
- Website visitors (30 days)
- Video viewers (50%+ completion, 30 days)
